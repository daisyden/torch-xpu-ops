## Root Cause

addmm belongs to the oneMKL-backed BLAS family in the operator registry at
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/.opencode/skills/bug_scrub/prepare_data/pytorch_xpu_backend_analysis/xpu_supported_operators_complete_list.md:172-181
and 814-817. The XPU BLAS implementation routes addmm_complex_out_xpu to the oneMKL path when
USE_ONEMKL_XPU is enabled at third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/ATen/native/xpu/Blas.cpp:356-360, and
BlasImpl.cpp ultimately calls oneapi::mkl::blas::column_major::gemm/gemm_batch at
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/ATen/native/xpu/mkl/BlasImpl.cpp:200-212. The issue reports a
quantified +0.0091 geomean R delta on G31 (>0.7%) across 62 models, indicating oneMKL GEMM shape
tuning/dispatch inefficiency for transformer inference shapes rather than a functional addmm
failure.

## Fix Approach

- Extract representative addmm M/N/K, dtype, transpose, and batch patterns from the affected transformer models and benchmark them directly against oneMKL GEMM.
- Tune shape routing, layout/transposition handling, and any pre/post-copy overhead in the XPU BLAS wrapper.
- escalate low-efficiency oneMKL kernels with reduced reproducer shapes to oneMKL and add regression microbenchmarks for the high-impact shapes.
