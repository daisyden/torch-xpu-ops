## Root Cause

The failing op is registered to XPU in
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/yaml/native/native_functions.yaml:3534-3542 and the wrapper calls
xpu::max_pool2d_with_indices_backward_kernel at
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/ATen/native/xpu/DilatedMaxPool2d.cpp:22-41. That kernel forces
contiguous copies of input, gradOutput, and indices, zeroes gradInput, then dispatches the SYCL
backward path at third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/ATen/native/xpu/sycl/DilatedMaxPool2d.cpp:1311-1317
and 1351-1457; the dependency lookup maps max_pool2d_with_indices_backward to a SYCL pooling kernel
at xpu_supported_operators_complete_list.md:849. The CI failure is in inductor tests, but the
issue's standalone eager-vs-CPU reproducer isolates fp16 numerical mismatch to this XPU backward
kernel rather than Triton/Inductor codegen.

## Fix Approach

- - - Fix third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/ATen/native/xpu/sycl/DilatedMaxPool2d.cpp, focusing on fp16 accumulation/index handling in MaxPool2dBackwardDeterministicKernelFunctor and the channels-last vector path. - add a targeted XPU regression for the [2,64,20,20], kernel 13/stride 1/padding 2/dilation 1 case and remove any temporary PyTorch skip after the kernel is corrected.
