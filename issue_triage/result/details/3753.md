## Root Cause

ProcessGroupXCCL records all_gather/_allgather_base directly through xccl::onecclAllGather at
src/xccl/ProcessGroupXCCL.cpp:1646 and src/xccl/ProcessGroupXCCL.cpp:1723 without a graph-safe XCCL
path. The failing FSDP CUDA graph capture reaches torch.distributed.fsdp foreach_all_gather and then
group._allgather_base, matching the local graph replay test at
test/xpu/distributed/test_c10d_ops_xccl.py:1056 where all_gather_into_tensor is captured and raises
'Graph nodes cannot depend on events from outside the graph.'

## Fix Approach

- Make XCCL collectives graph-capture-safe: update the ProcessGroupXCCL collective path to avoid dependencies on events created outside the active XPUGraph, or add a graph-aware path/guard for _allgather_base and list all_gather.
- Cover the fix with test_allgather_graph_replay and the FSDP TestFullyShardCudaGraph reproducer before re-enabling the skipped distributed case.
