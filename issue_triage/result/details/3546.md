## Root Cause

The failing test constructs jagged NestedTensor q/k/v through a packed projection in
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/test/xpu/test_nestedtensor_xpu.py:7167-7191 and calls
F.scaled_dot_product_attention on non-contiguous transposed tensors. The jagged SDPA selector in
torch/nested/_internal/sdpa.py tries flash, efficient, math, then cuDNN at lines 293-337; math
rejects these inputs because q/k/v are not contiguous after transpose at lines 273-283. XPU flash
attention also cannot cover this path today: the XPU flash gate includes check_nested_tensor in
aten/src/ATen/native/transformers/xpu/sdp_utils.cpp:165-181, and torch-xpu-ops' flash varlen path is
explicitly unimplemented in
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/ATen/native/transformers/xpu/flash_attn/flash_api.cpp:65-108.

## Fix Approach

- - - Implement the XPU jagged/varlen SDPA backend rather than relying on the math fallback. - Complete the varlen flash attention forward path in torch-xpu-ops src/ATen/native/transformers/xpu/flash_attn/flash_api.cpp, relax the XPU nested-tensor flash constraints to mirror CUDA's nested branch once the kernel exists, and wire the XPU dispatch/selector so jagged_scaled_dot_product_attention can choose the XPU flash backend for this NestedTensor case.
