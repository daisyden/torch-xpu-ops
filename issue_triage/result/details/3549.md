## Root Cause

The skipped XPU activation-checkpointing tests exercise functionalize_rng_ops=True in
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/test/xpu/dynamo/test_higher_order_ops_xpu.py:6903-6948 and expect
rngprims.philox_rand in the graph at lines 6918-6925. The functional RNG decompositions reject XPU
before lowering: torch/_decomp/decompositions_for_rng.py:33-40 and 54-62 call throw_on_non_cuda for
rand/rand_like, while torch/_prims/rng_prims.py:117-124 rejects any non-CUDA device and uses
CUDARngStateHelper. The helper itself is CUDA-only at torch/_prims_common/__init__.py:2192-2215, and
philox_rand_offset hardcodes torch.cuda device properties at torch/_prims/rng_prims.py:57-76.

## Fix Approach

- - - - Fix in pytorch/pytorch by generalizing the functional RNG path from CUDA-only to Philox-capable accelerators. - Replace the non-CUDA gates in torch/_decomp/decompositions_for_rng.py and torch/_prims/rng_prims.py with a backend capability check including xpu, introduce/use a device-agnostic RNG state helper for seed/offset, and make philox_rand_offset compute a safe XPU offset from torch.xpu device properties or a conservative backend-independent bound.
