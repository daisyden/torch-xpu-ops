## Root Cause

FlashAttention forward launch hard-codes SIMD16 with 256-GRF kernel properties at
src/ATen/native/transformers/xpu/flash_attn/sycltla/mha_fwd_launch.h:79 and selects the spilling
192/256-head-dim FMHA tile shapes at
src/ATen/native/transformers/xpu/flash_attn/sycltla/mha_fwd_launch.h:353 and
src/ATen/native/transformers/xpu/flash_attn/sycltla/mha_fwd_launch.h:368. Those configurations
instantiate XeFMHAFwdKernel via
src/ATen/native/transformers/xpu/flash_attn/sycltla/mha_fwd_launch.h:243, matching the compiler
warnings about small register spills on PVC.

## Fix Approach

- Retune the forward FMHA PVC tile configurations: evaluate smaller Q/PV/output tiles, fewer V tiles, different subgroup layout, or a non-256-GRF launch property for the spilling variants.
- Compare compiler spill reports and latency for the affected causal/non-causal head-dim cases, then keep the best variant behind the existing headdim dispatch with targeted flash-attention performance coverage.
