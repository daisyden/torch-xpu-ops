## Root Cause

18 rerun-confirmed Dynamo/Inductor latency drops on BMG after upgrading driver 25.40->26.19 across
torchbench/timm. Persisting after verification means not benchmark noise; the common factor is the
driver upgrade, indicating a backend/runtime interaction with Inductor-generated kernels (perf
regression). P0 per the >7% perf-regression rule given confirmed drops.

## Fix Approach

- Treat as a driver regression.
- open a joint investigation with the Intel driver team using a minimized set of worst-affected benchmarks.
- Quantify per-model deltas, prioritize >7% drops, collect kernel-level evidence (timeline, kernel selection, occupancy/memory) comparing 25.40 vs 26.19 to isolate the regression signature.
- Short-term: pin/recommend 25.40 for affected workloads.
- validate the driver fix with the same rerun suite before lifting mitigation.
