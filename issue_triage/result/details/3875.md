## Root Cause

End-to-end timm_models regression in nightly vs PyTorch 2.12 release on PVC with Inductor, pointing
to a compiler/runtime perf change, not a model-level functional bug. Most likely upstream Inductor
codegen/scheduling drift after the 2.12 cut, with possible secondary sensitivity to the bundled
Triton path. No evidence torch-xpu-ops operator correctness changed.

## Fix Approach

- Commit-window bisect from v2.12 to the affected nightly on the timm_models PVC E2E suite, isolating PyTorch and Triton revisions separately.
- collect kernel-level timing diffs (fusion decisions, launch counts, codegen) for top regressed models.
- Once the offending upstream change is found, apply a targeted Inductor heuristic/scheduling guard (or temp backend config) and validate recovery vs the 2.12 baseline.
