## Root Cause

The affected tests capture and replay ProcessGroup XCCL collectives inside torch.xpu.XPUGraph:
all_reduce at third_party/torch-xpu-ops/test/xpu/distributed/test_c10d_ops_xccl.py:1009-1038,
all_gather_into_tensor at lines 1042-1073, and reduce_scatter_tensor at lines 1077-1108. The XCCL
implementation explicitly gates collective capture on oneCCL graph-capture support in
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/xccl/ProcessGroupXCCL.cpp:151-167, then creates/uses oneCCL streams
during collective enqueue at lines 757-812 and avoids status updates during capture at lines
888-897. Because the issue says these must be skipped until DLE 2026.0, the root dependency is the
XCCL/oneCCL graph-capture runtime rather than test logic or an aten kernel.

## Fix Approach

- - - - Short term, filter these three distributed graph replay cases in the torch-xpu-ops distributed skip list used by op_ut until the CI image upgrades to DLE/oneCCL 2026.0. - Long term, after the DLE upgrade, re-enable the tests and validate ProcessGroupXCCL::collective graph capture/replay paths in src/xccl/ProcessGroupXCCL.cpp for all_reduce, all_gather_into_tensor, and reduce_scatter_tensor.
