## Root Cause

XPU unary complex kernels gate complex dispatch with AT_DISPATCH_COMPLEX_TYPES_AND(kComplexHalf,
...), which includes complex64/128 + complex half but not kBComplex32. So
abs/conj/real/imag/angle/neg on kBComplex32 tensors do not select a valid XPU kernel and fail UT
coverage. A dtype-dispatch omission, not an algorithmic error.

## Fix Approach

- Update the XPU SYCL unary complex dispatch sites to include kBComplex32 in AbsKernel and UnaryComplexKernels (conj/real/imag/angle/neg paths), mirroring existing complex dtype handling, and keep duplicated vendor-tree codepaths consistent.
- Add/extend UTs for these unary ops on kBComplex32 to verify kernel selection and numerical correctness on XPU.
