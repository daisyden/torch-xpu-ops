## Root Cause

Upstream added new reduction ops linalg__powsum and _foreach_powsum.Scalar, but their dispatch
tables include only CPU/CUDA (CUDA-only fastpath for foreach) with no XPU registration. XPU lacks a
backend-specific path, so support is functionally incomplete and may fall back to non-optimized
composite behavior or miss XPU kernel coverage.
