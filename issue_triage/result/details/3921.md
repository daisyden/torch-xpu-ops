test_dispatch_symbolic_meta_outplace_all_strides. Upstream has this expectedFailure decoration for

corresponding XPU decoration. So torch-xpu-ops runs the symbolic meta all-strides variant on XPU and
reports a known upstream behavior gap instead of an expected failure.
