## Root Cause

The accuracy collapse occurs only in PT2E INT8 Inductor quantization on XPU for shufflenet_v2_x1_0,
while FP32 remains healthy and a later comment reports the issue no longer reproduces on torch 2.13.
The implicated lowering is the same oneDNN-backed XPU qconv path: SUPPORTED_MKLDNN_DEVICES includes
xpu in torch/_inductor/utils.py:4118, QConvPointWisePT2E emits
aoti_torch_xpu__qconv_pointwise_tensor in torch/_inductor/mkldnn_ir.py:598-607 and prepares
convolution fusion at lines 646-680, and the INT8 path converts ConstantBuffer weight zero points to
int32 in torch/_inductor/mkldnn_lowerings.py:756-764. The observed TensorMeta warning matches
torch/_inductor/select_algorithm.py:3278-3284, where GPU extern choice metadata construction
failures are swallowed, making a wrong layout/kernel choice plausible for grouped/depthwise INT8
convs.
