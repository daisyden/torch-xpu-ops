## Root Cause

XPU core tensor-op kernels are missing kBComplex32 in their dtype dispatch paths, especially copy
and advanced indexing (incl masked_fill/index_put dispatch sites). These ops cannot instantiate the
required SYCL kernels for kBComplex32 even though they are fundamental ops expected to mirror CUDA.
A dtype-enablement omission, not a one-off logic bug.

## Fix Approach

- Enable kBComplex32 across the affected XPU dispatch macros and kernel specializations in CopyKernel and Indexing paths, including all referenced indexing/masked_fill/index_put dispatch sites.
- Ensure type conversion/copy paths accept kBComplex32 end-to-end.
- add/extend UT coverage for copy, indexing, masked_fill, and index_put on kBComplex32 to prevent regressions.
