## Root Cause

XPU unary geometric kernels for complex trig dispatch with
AT_DISPATCH_COMPLEX_TYPES_AND(kComplexHalf, ...), not enabling the new kBComplex32 path. So
sin/cos/tan (and related complex geometric kernels split from #4082) don't cover this dtype in XPU
UTs. A dtype-enablement gap in SYCL kernel dispatch, not an algorithmic correctness issue.

## Fix Approach

- Add kBComplex32 in the XPU SYCL UnaryGeometric*Kernel dispatch macros (start with UnaryGeometricSinKernel.cpp.
- apply same to Cos/Tan and related complex trig/hyperbolic kernels), verify opmath_type/functor casting compiles for the dtype, and add UT coverage for complex32 sin/cos/tan on XPU.
