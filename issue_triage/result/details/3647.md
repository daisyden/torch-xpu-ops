## Root Cause

The Linux op_ut workflow currently redirects stderr from run_test_with_skip.py exclusively into
op_ut_with_skip_test_error.log while only stdout is piped through tee to the console and
op_ut_with_skip_test.log at third_party/torch-xpu-ops/.github/actions/linux-uttest/action.yml:61-64.
The same split pattern is used for op_extended at
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/.github/actions/linux-uttest/action.yml:49-50 and skipped_ut at
third_party/torch-xpu-ops/.github/actions/linux-uttest/action.yml:76-78, so CI console logs can miss
stderr-only failures and the separate files lack interleaving/timestamps for correlating messages to
tests.

## Fix Approach

- - - - Update third_party/torch-xpu-ops/.github/actions/linux-uttest/action.yml to either merge stderr into stdout before tee or tee stdout and stderr separately while preserving stderr to the GitHub console. - apply the same logging policy to op_ut, skipped_ut, and op_extended, and confirm artifacts still include the expected *_test.log and *_test_error.log files if retained.
