## Root Cause

test_Conv2d_groups_nobias accuracy failure on XPU. Grouped Conv2d on XPU is dispatched through
oneDNN (third_party/torch-xpu-ops/src/ATen/native/xpu/Convolution.cpp) and the no-bias grouped path
produces results that exceed the upstream tolerance, indicating a regression or precision mismatch
in the oneDNN grouped-conv primitive used for this configuration.
