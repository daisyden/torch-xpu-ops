## Root Cause

Release-to-release E2E performance regression on PVC with many Inductor ratios ~0.64-0.90 (well over
7% slowdown vs 2.12). Broad across torchbench/timm/huggingface and multiple precisions, while eager
is often near 1.0 - pointing to a compiler-path regression rather than a device/runtime collapse.
Most likely upstream PyTorch Inductor changes between 2.12 and 2.13.

## Fix Approach

- Mark P0 and bisect upstream PyTorch between the 2.12 and 2.13 ranges using worst-drop models (mobilenet_v2 training, XLNetLMHeadModel, inception_v3) under identical PVC settings.
- Collect per-model compile/runtime breakdowns (graph breaks, fusion counts, generated kernel counts, autotune choices) and compare eager vs inductor deltas to isolate regressing Inductor passes.
- If a hotspot maps to generated Triton kernels, open a Triton follow-up with a minimized repro.
- otherwise route to upstream Inductor owners with the bisected culprit commit.
