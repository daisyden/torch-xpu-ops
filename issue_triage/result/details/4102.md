## Root Cause

torch.stft gradient (grad/gradgrad/fwgrad_bwgrad) with complex128 on XPU causes the Level Zero GPU
driver to report device-lost (UR_RESULT_ERROR_DEVICE_LOST) during the spectral op. The device-lost
is a driver/runtime-level failure surfaced from the stft gradient path; stft itself is oneMKL-backed
but the crash is at the Level Zero driver layer. (Agent retry hit a transient 499; classification
from the issue body.)

## Fix Approach

- Reproduce stft grad with complex128 on BMG and isolate whether the device-lost originates in the oneMKL FFT call or the autograd backward path.
- collect a Level Zero/driver log.
- Escalate the device-lost to Intel driver owners with a minimized repro, and add a guarded skip/xfail for stft complex128 gradcheck on affected BMG+driver combos until the driver fix lands.
